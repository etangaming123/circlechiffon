CiRCLE Chiffon

A Discord bot for maimai DX NET, SEGA's web service for the arcade rhythm game maimai DX. Link your SEGA ID account and view your profile, recent plays, best 50, CiRCLE (team), etc. (or just use other bots lmfao)

Features


  • Account Linking

    Link your SEGA ID to view your profile, recent plays, and b50 rating right in Discord.


  • Best50 Rating Images

    Renders your Best-50 rating image, powered by dxrating.net


  • Song Lookup & Rating Calculator

    Look up any song's chart levels and details, or calculate rating points from a constant and achievement% - no account needed. Also powered by dxrating.net.
