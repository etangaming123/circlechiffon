    Quick start

    1. Pick a render at the top: Best 50, Profile core or Profile extra. Best 50 also has a Card view for the one layout shared by all 50 cards.
    2. Load your art in Template: a Base image (drawn under the render) and/or a Top image (drawn over it). Get a guide PNG from /cc-template-get to draw over.
    3. Move things. Drag an element, pull its handles to resize, or type exact numbers in Inspector. Hide what you don't want with the checkbox in Elements.
    4. Style it. Opacity for any element; colour and outline for text. Template holds layout-wide colours and options.
    5. Check it in Live. Switch the canvas from Edit to Live to see placeholder text (999,999, 100.5000%, a very long chart name) over your art.
    6. Export layout.json, then run /cc-template-upload with it and your images. Verify the real thing with /cc-template-preview.

    Good to know

    • Nothing is uploaded. Images and layouts stay in your browser. Your layout autosaves here for each render.
    • Live is approximate. Fonts and sizes are close, not identical. Image elements (icon, jacket, badges) show as tinted blocks. /cc-template-preview is the truth.
    • Dashed outline = follows. Some elements sit right after another (the rating badge follows the name). Dragging one sideways, or typing an x, pins it.
    • Snap in the canvas toolbar rounds drags to a grid (0 = off).
    • Import an exported layout.json to keep working on it later.
    New here? Drag elements around, then export layout.json. Everything stays in your browser.
